## | আমরা সবসময় মনে রাখি  |  We Always Remember |  

<font color="brown">
	
- যখন একজন মানুষ মারা যায়,  তার সব কাজ শেষ হয়ে যায় , তিনটি ব্যতিক্রম ছাড়া: সদকা জারিয়া (চলমান দান) , এমন জ্ঞান যা থেকে উপকার পাওয়া যায়, এবং একটি সৎ সন্তান যে তার জন্য দোয়া করে ।  | মুহাম্মদ (সাঃ) ; হাদিস - সহিহ মুসলিম | When a person dies, his works end, except for three: ongoing charity, knowledge that is benefited from, and a righteous child who prays for him. | Prophet Mohammed (PBUH); Hadith - Sahih Muslim |

- যে ব্যক্তি ভাল কাজের জন্য সুপারিশ করবে, তার জন্য তাতে (সাওয়াবের) অংশ আছে এবং যে মন্দ কাজের জন্য সুপারিশ করবে, তার জন্য তাতে অংশ আছে । আর আল্লাহ সব কিছুর উপর নজর রাখেন । | সূরাঃ ৪ ,  আন-নিসা  , আয়াতঃ : ৮৫  | Whoever intercedes for a good cause will have a reward therefrom; and whoever intercedes for an evil cause will have a burden therefrom. And ever is Allah, over all things, a Keeper.  | Surah 4 , An-Nisa ,  Verse: 85 |

</font>

**In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful\
আল্লাহর নামে, যিনি পরম করুণাময়, পরম দয়ালু**

------------------------------------------------------------


আল্লাহর সুন্দর ৯৯টি নাম, যা আসমা উল-হুসনা নামে পরিচিত, তাঁর অসীম গুণাবলির প্রকাশ এবং তাঁর মহান সত্তার প্রতিফলন । প্রতিটি নাম তাঁর অনন্ত করুণা, সুবিচার, এবং সর্বজ্ঞতার পরিচয় বহন করে ।

The beautiful 99 names of Allah, known as Asma ul-Husna, are a manifestation of His boundless qualities and a reflection of His magnificent essence. Each name embodies His infinite mercy, perfect justice, and all-encompassing knowledge.

-----------------------------------------------------------------

<font color="blue">

1. আর-রহমান | Ar-Rahman (The Beneficent) - Allah’s mercy extends to all of creation.
2. আর-রহিম | Ar-Rahim (The Merciful) – Allah’s special mercy for believers.
3. আল-মালিক | Al-Malik (The King) – Allah’s sovereignty over the universe.
4. আল-কুদ্দুস  | Al-Quddus (The Most Sacred) – Allah is pure and free from any imperfection.
5. আস-সালাম | As-Salam (The Source of Peace) – Allah is the source of all peace and safety.
6. আল-মু’মিন | Al-Mu’min (The Guarantor) – Allah grants security and peace.
7. আল-মুহাইমিন | Al-Muhaymin (The Guardian) – Allah oversees and protects everything.
8. আল-আজিজ | Al-Aziz (The Almighty) – Allah is powerful and victorious.
9. আল-জাব্বার  | Al-Jabbar (The Compeller) – Allah overcomes all obstacles.
10. আল-মুতাকাব্বির | Al-Mutakabbir (The Supreme) – Allah is above all weaknesses.
11. আল-খালিক | Al-Khaliq (The Creator) – Allah creates all things from nothing.
12. আল-বারি | Al-Bari (The Evolver) – Allah shapes and forms everything uniquely.
13. আল-মুসাওয়ির | Al-Musawwir (The Fashioner) – Allah gives each creation its form.
14. আল-গফফার | Al-Ghaffar (The Great Forgiver) – Allah forgives repeatedly.
15. আল-কাহহার | Al-Qahhar (The Subduer) – Allah subdues all things under His will.
16. আল-ওয়াহহাব  | Al-Wahhab (The Bestower) – Allah freely gives gifts and blessings.
17. আর-রাজ্জাক  | Ar-Razzaq (The Provider) – Allah provides sustenance for all creation.
18. আল-ফাত্তাহ | Al-Fattah (The Opener) – Allah opens paths and solutions.
19. আল-আলিম  | Al-Alim (The All-Knowing) – Allah has full knowledge of everything.
20. আল-কাবিদ  | Al-Qabid (The Withholder) – Allah can withhold provision or blessings.
21. আল-বাসিত | Al-Basit (The Extender) – Allah grants abundant provision.
22. আল-খাফিদ | Al-Khafid (The Reducer) – Allah can lower status or position.
23. আর-রাফি’ | Ar-Rafi (The Exalter) – Allah elevates those He wills.
24. আল-মুইজ্জ | Al-Mu’izz (The Honor-Giver) – Allah grants honor and dignity.
25. আল-মুযিল | Al-Mudhill (The Dishonorer) – Allah can humble the proud.
26. আস-সামি’ | As-Sami (The All-Hearing) – Allah hears everything, spoken or silent.
27. আল-বাসির  | Al-Basir (The All-Seeing) – Allah sees all things.
28. আল-হাকাম  | Al-Hakam (The Judge) – Allah is the ultimate judge of all matters.
29. আল-আদল  | Al-Adl (The Just) – Allah is absolutely fair and just.
30. আল-লতিফ | Al-Latif (The Subtle One) – Allah works in ways beyond perception.
31. আল-খবির | Al-Khabir (The All-Aware) – Allah is fully aware of inner realities.
32. আল-হালিম  | Al-Halim (The Forbearing) – Allah is patient and does not hasten punishment.
33. আল-আজিম  | Al-Azim (The Magnificent) – Allah is grand and majestic.
34. আল-গফুর  | Al-Ghafur (The Forgiving) – Allah forgives and covers sins.
35. আশ-শাকুর | Ash-Shakur (The Appreciative) – Allah rewards even small deeds.
36. আল-আলি | Al-Ali (The Most High) – Allah is exalted above all.
37. আল-কবির | Al-Kabir (The Most Great) – Allah’s greatness is beyond comprehension.
38. আল-হাফিজ | Al-Hafiz (The Preserver) – Allah protects and preserves everything.
39. আল-মুকিত  | Al-Muqit (The Sustainer) – Allah provides nourishment for all.
40. আল-হাসিব  | Al-Hasib (The Reckoner) – Allah takes account of every deed.
41. আল-জলিল  | Al-Jalil (The Majestic) – Allah is full of majesty and honor.
42. আল-করিম | Al-Karim (The Generous) – Allah is kind and generous.
43. আর-রকিব | Ar-Raqib (The Watchful) – Allah watches over everything.
44. আল-মুজিব | Al-Mujib (The Responsive) – Allah answers prayers and calls.
45. আল-ওয়াসি’ | Al-Wasi (The All-Encompassing) – Allah’s knowledge and mercy extend to all.
46. আল-হাকিম | Al-Hakim (The All-Wise) – Allah has the wisdom in all actions.
47. আল-ওয়াদুদ | Al-Wadud (The Loving One) – Allah is full of love and kindness.
48. আল-মাজিদ | Al-Majid (The Glorious) – Allah is full of glory and honor.
49. আল-বা’ইস | Al-Baith (The Resurrector) – Allah brings the dead to life.
50. আশ-শাহিদ | Ash-Shahid (The Witness) – Allah witnesses all things.
51. আল-হক | Al-Haqq (The Truth) – Allah is the ultimate reality.
52. আল-ওকিল | Al-Wakil (The Trustee) – Allah is the reliable disposer of affairs.
53. আল-কাউই | Al-Qawiyy (The Strong) – Allah has absolute strength.
54. আল-মাতিন | Al-Matin (The Firm) – Allah is steadfast and unshakable.
55. আল-ওয়ালি | Al-Wali (The Protector) – Allah protects and guards.
56. আল-হামিদ | Al-Hamid (The Praiseworthy) – Allah deserves all praise.
57. আল-মুহসি | Al-Muhsi (The Appraiser) – Allah knows the count of all things.
58. আল-মুবদি | Al-Mubdi (The Originator) – Allah originates creation.
59. আল-মুইদ | Al-Muid (The Restorer) – Allah restores life and recreates.
60. আল-মুহই | Al-Muhyi (The Giver of Life) – Allah grants life.
61. আল-মুমিত | Al-Mumit (The Taker of Life) – Allah causes death.
62. আল-হাই | Al-Hayy (The Ever-Living) – Allah is ever-living and undying.
63. আল-কাইয়ুম | Al-Qayyum (The Sustainer) – Allah sustains and manages all.
64. আল-ওয়াজিদ | Al-Wajid (The Finder) – Allah finds all and lacks nothing.
65. আল-মাজিদ | Al-Majid (The Noble) – Allah is noble and honored.
66. আল-ওয়াহিদ | Al-Wahid (The One) – Allah is one without partners.
67. আল-আহাদ | Al-Ahad (The Unique) – Allah is uniquely one.
68. আস-সামাদ | As-Samad (The Eternal) – Allah is self-sufficient and independent.
69. আল-কাদির | Al-Qadir (The Omnipotent) – Allah has power over all.
70. আল-মুক্বতাদির | Al-Muqtadir (The Determiner) – Allah has control over destiny.
71. আল-মুক্বাদ্দিম | Al-Muqaddim (The Expediter) – Allah brings forward whomever He wills.
72. আল-মু'আখখির | Al-Mu’akhkhir (The Delayer) – Allah can delay things for a reason.
73. আল-আউওয়াল | Al-Awwal (The First) – Allah existed before everything.
74. আল-আখির | Al-Akhir (The Last) – Allah remains after all ends.
75. আল-জহির | Az-Zahir (The Manifest) – Allah’s existence is evident in creation.
76. আল-বাতিন | Al-Batin (The Hidden) – Allah’s essence is beyond human understanding.
77. আল-ওয়ালি | Al-Wali (The Patron) – Allah is the helper of the believers.
78. আল-মুতাআলি | Al-Muta’ali (The Self-Exalted) – Allah is exalted beyond comprehension.
79. আল-বার | Al-Barr (The Beneficent) – Allah is good to all.
80. আত-তাওয়াব | At-Tawwab (The Acceptor of Repentance) – Allah accepts sincere repentance.
81. আল-মুনতাকিম | Al-Muntaqim (The Avenger) – Allah’s justice reaches those who deserve it.
82. আল-আফুউ | Al-Afuww (The Pardoner) – Allah forgives and erases sins.
83. আর-রউফ | Ar-Ra’uf (The Most Kind) – Allah is full of kindness and compassion.
84. মালিকুল মুলক | Malik al-Mulk (Master of the Kingdom) – Allah owns all realms.
85. যুল-জালাল ওয়াল-ইকরাম | Dhul-Jalal wa-l-Ikram (Lord of Majesty and Honor) – Allah is full of majesty.
86. আল-মুক্বসিত | Al-Muqsit (The Just One) – Allah establishes justice.
87. আল-জামি’ | Al-Jami (The Gatherer) – Allah will gather all for judgment.
88. আল-গনি | Al-Ghani (The Self-Sufficient) – Allah is free of need.
89. আল-মুগনি | Al-Mughni (The Enricher) – Allah enriches whomever He wills.
90. আল-মানি’ | Al-Mani (The Preventer) – Allah can prevent harm.
91. আদ-দার | Ad-Darr (The Distresser) – Allah allows hardship as a test.
92. আন-নাফি | An-Nafi (The Benefactor) – Allah brings benefit to His creation.
93. আন-নুর | An-Nur (The Light) – Allah is the source of guidance.
94. আল-হাদি | Al-Hadi (The Guide) – Allah guides hearts to truth.
95. আল-বাদি’ | Al-Badi (The Incomparable) – Allah’s creation has no equal.
96. আল-বাকি | Al-Baqi (The Ever-Surviving) – Allah remains when all else perishes.
97. আল-ওয়ারিস | Al-Warith (The Inheritor) – Allah will inherit the earth and everything in it.
98. আর-রশিদ | Ar-Rashid (The Guide to the Right Path) – Allah directs to the path of virtue.
99. আস-সবুর | As-Sabur (The Patient) – Allah is patient, delaying punishment for repentance.

</font>

Each of these names reflects Allah’s unique and perfect attributes, helping believers understand His nature and relate to Him in worship.
