**Site Grading & Drainage Plan :**

The plan will ensure, at a minimum, the following:

- Minimize cut-and-fill volumes to achieve cost-effective and balanced grading.
- Ensure positive overland flow is maintained across the site for effective drainage.
- Design the grading plan to promote smooth and controlled overland flow.
- Prevent adverse impacts on neighboring properties by managing grading and drainage effectively.
- Limit surface ponding to a maximum depth of 0.30 m, ensuring alignment with the site’s drainage and servicing plan
