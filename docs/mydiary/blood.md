**In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful\

----

Blood is a vital component of the human body and all living beings. Blood tests cover various health areas, and the main purpose is to ensure that all organs, such as the kidneys, thyroid, and liver...., as well as indicators of conditions like diabetes and cholesterol levels, are functioning properly. If any abnormalities are found, doctors will diagnose them based on the results.

It is incredible how much information doctors can gather from a simple blood test. We should appreciate the doctors and scientists who dedicate their lives to understanding the human body and finding ways to treat diseases. Their tireless efforts deserve deep respect and gratitude.

However, the wisdom and planning of Allah when creating humanity is often overlooked. Allah knew that mankind would face health challenges and would need treatment to live fulfilling lives. Blood is designed in such a way that it allows for the detection of diseases through simple tests, serving as a reminder of Allah’s mercy and brilliance in His creation. This reflects how Allah cares for creation by providing tools to identify and treat illnesses, enabling healthier lives. As stated in the Quran: <font color="blue"> “And We have certainly created man and We know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than his jugular vein.” - (Surah Qaf, 50:16).   </font> This verse reminds us that Allah knows us intimately and is aware of our needs. The ability to identify health issues through blood tests highlights Allah’s mercy in providing us with the means to detect and treat illnesses, enabling us to live healthier lives. We should always reflect on His perfect design and express our gratitude for the knowledge He has given us through doctors and scientists.
