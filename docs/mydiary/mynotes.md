# Time


Time is a test. Every second is a moment where we have the chance to make decisions—good or bad. Allah tests us through the passage of time, observing how we act in different stages of life: in youth, adulthood, and old age. The changing seasons and the passage of years remind us that this life is temporary, and time will eventually run out.


We are constantly moving through life’s stages, from birth to death, and time is a silent witness to all our actions. We are given a limited amount of Time, and how we spend it determines our success or failure in the Hereafter.

# Blood Test



Today, I had a blood test as part of my annual routine check-up. This test covers various health areas. While I'm not a doctor, I understand that the main purpose of a blood test is to ensure that all organs in my body, such as the kidneys, thyroid, and liver, as well as indicators of conditions like diabetes and cholesterol levels, are functioning properly. If any abnormalities are found, the doctor will diagnose them based on the results.

I’ve never given it much thought before, but it’s incredible how much information doctors can gather from a simple blood test. We should appreciate the doctors and scientists who dedicate their lives to understanding the human body and finding ways to treat diseases. I have deep respect and gratitude for their tireless efforts.

However, we often overlook the wisdom and planning of Allah when He created us. He knew that mankind would face health challenges and would need treatment to live fulfilling lives. Blood is a vital component of the human body and all living beings. Allah designed our blood in such a way that we can detect diseases through simple tests. This serves as a reminder of Allah’s mercy and brilliance in His creation.

This is how Allah shows His care for us, providing the tools to identify and treat illnesses, allowing us to lead healthier lives. As it says in the Quran: <font color="blue"> “And We have certainly created man and We know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than his jugular vein.” - (Surah Qaf, 50:16).  </font>  This verse reminds us that Allah knows us intimately and is aware of our needs. The ability to identify health issues through blood tests highlights Allah’s mercy in providing us with the means to detect and treat illnesses, enabling us to live healthier lives. We should always reflect on His perfect design and express our gratitude for the knowledge He has given us through doctors and scientists.
