1. Prophet Abraham (Ibrahim) – Known as the patriarch of many prophets, including Ishmael and Isaac.

2. Prophet Ishmael (Isma’il) – Son of Abraham, associated with the lineage leading to Prophet Muhammad.

3. Prophet Isaac (Ishaq) – Son of Abraham, father of Jacob (Israel).

4. Prophet Jacob (Yaqub) – Son of Isaac, also known as Israel, the father of the twelve tribes of Israel.

5. Prophet Joseph (Yusuf) – Son of Jacob, known for his story in Egypt.

6. Prophet Moses (Musa) – A central figure in the Torah, he led the Israelites out of Egypt.

7. Prophet Aaron (Harun) – Brother of Moses, served as his assistant and the high priest.

8. Prophet David (Dawud) – Known for his kingship and receiving the Psalms (Zabur).

9. Prophet Solomon (Sulayman) – Son of David, known for his wisdom and rule over the jinn and animals.

10. Prophet Zachariah (Zakariya) – A righteous priest and the father of John the Baptist.

11. Prophet John (Yahya) – Known in Christianity as John the Baptist, he prepared the way for Jesus.

12. Prophet Jesus (Isa) – Known in Christianity as Jesus Christ, a significant prophet in Islam.

13. Prophet Muhammad – The final prophet in Islam, to whom the Quran was revealed.
