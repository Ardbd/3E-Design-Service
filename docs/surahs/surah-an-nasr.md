Surah An-Nasr is the 110th chapter of the Quran, revealed in Makkah. The name "An-Nasr" means "The Help." This Surah is a short but significant chapter that focuses on the help and support of Allah. It celebrates the victory and success granted by Allah to the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and the early Muslim community.

The Surah highlights the importance of recognizing and thanking Allah for His help and support. It serves as a reminder of the divine assistance that comes during times of success and victory, and the appropriate response to such blessings.


-----------

## Verses from the Quran


**Translet (Saiah International)**

<font color="blue">

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.


1. When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest,
2. And you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes,
3. Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance.

</font>

---------------

## What Allah Says

Surah An-Nasr consists of 3 verses. It begins by announcing that Allah's help and victory have come and that the people are entering the religion of Allah in large numbers. This is seen as a sign of success and divine support for the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and his mission.

The Surah then advises the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) to praise and thank Allah and seek His forgiveness. It highlights the importance of acknowledging Allah’s role in the successes achieved and maintaining humility and gratitude.

---------------

## Lessons


**Reflection on Allah's Help and Victory:** Surah An-Nasr reminds us of the importance of recognizing Allah’s help and support in our achievements. It teaches us to be thankful and to attribute our successes to Allah.


**Reflection on Gratitude and Humility:** The Surah emphasizes the need to be humble and grateful when we experience success and victory. It teaches us that we should not become arrogant but instead turn to Allah with praise and seek His forgiveness.
